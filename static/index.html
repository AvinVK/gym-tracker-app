Menstrual Cycle Phases

🩸

Menstrual Phase

Day 1-5

The uterine lining sheds and bleeding occurs. Energy is often lower — lighter workouts like walking or yoga can feel better than high-intensity training.

🌱

Follicular Phase

Day 6-13

Estrogen rises as the body prepares to release an egg. Energy and strength typically climb — a good window to push a little harder in training.

🥚

Ovulation Phase

Day 14

An egg is released — often the peak of energy and strength in the cycle. A good window for harder sessions or chasing a PR.

🌙

Luteal Phase

Day 15-28

Progesterone rises, then drops toward the end of the phase — PMS symptoms and lower energy are common near the end. Moderate, steady training tends to work best.
