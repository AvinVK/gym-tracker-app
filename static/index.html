Menstrual Cycle Phases

🩸

Menstrual Phase

Day 1-5

The uterine lining sheds and bleeding occurs.

🌱

Follicular Phase

Day 6-13

Estrogen rises as the body prepares to release an egg.

🥚

Ovulation Phase

Day 14

An egg is released.

🌙

Luteal Phase

Day 15-28

Progesterone rises, then drops toward the end of the phase — PMS symptoms are common near the end.
