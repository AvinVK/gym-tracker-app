How Streaks Work

  • Weekly, not daily. Log at least 4 days in a week and that week counts toward your streak.
  • Your week, your days. Your streak week runs on a 7-day cycle starting from the weekday you first logged a visit - not a fixed calendar week.
  • Shields protect you. Fall short in a week and a shield automatically covers it, holding your streak steady instead of resetting it to zero (it won't go up that week, since nothing was logged - but it won't drop either).
  • Earning shields. Everyone starts with 0. Reach a 3-week streak to earn your first shield, then another every 4 weeks after that, up to a max of 3.
  • Period weeks are covered. If a week overlaps your period (set in Your Cycle), a shortfall that week is covered automatically - free, no shield spent.

Menstrual Cycle Phases

Menstrual Phase

Day 1-5

The uterine lining sheds and bleeding occurs.

Follicular Phase

Day 6-13

Estrogen rises as the body prepares to release an egg.

Ovulation Phase

Day 14

An egg is released.

Luteal Phase

Day 15-28

Progesterone rises, then drops toward the end of the phase — PMS symptoms are common near the end.

Cycle Length

Days from the start of one period to the next. Most cycles run 21-35 days.

Your Privacy

  • We collect what you choose to log - workouts, energy, meals, and (if you turn it on) your period dates.
  • It's used only to show your own stats back to you - things like cycle phase and how it lines up with your training. It's never sold or shared with third parties or advertisers.
  • Want it gone? Turn off cycle tracking in your profile to stop logging new period dates, or contact support to have your data deleted.
